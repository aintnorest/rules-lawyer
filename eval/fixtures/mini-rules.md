# Mini TTRPG Rules
## Core Mechanic
When you attempt a risky action, roll 1d20 and add your Skill. If the total is 15 or higher, you succeed. On a natural 20, you critically succeed. On a natural 1, you critically fail.

## Combat
In combat, you have one Standard Action and one Move Action.
Attacking costs a Standard Action.

## Magic
Casting a spell costs a Standard Action and 1 Mana.
Spells:
- Fireball: Deals 3d6 damage.
- Shield: Adds +2 to your defense until your next turn.

## Mana Economy
Mana is a resource pool tracked on your character sheet. Unless a rule says otherwise, casting any spell from this book costs exactly 1 Mana per spell.

## Spell Appendix
Supplemental spell blocks in this section refer to the Mana Economy: each cast spends Mana from your pool the same way as core spells.

## Multiple Conditions
If you gain a condition you already have, there is usually no additional effect, but you must remove each instance separately to clear it completely.

## Fatigued Appendix
Fatigued is a debilitating condition described in Combat. For overlapping conditions, apply the rules under Multiple Conditions.
